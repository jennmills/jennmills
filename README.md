# Jennifer Mills

I design developer tools and AI experiences at Salesforce. I work across product direction, interaction design, and implementation.

I'm interested in how people stay in control as agents take on more work: knowing what is happening, when to step in, and how to recover.

I explore ideas in prototypes, turn useful patterns into shared components, and follow them into the product. I also help other designers work this way, with room to own their decisions and a consistent standard for the details.

Based in Vancouver, Canada.
